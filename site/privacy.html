JBLControl

Privacy Policy

Last updated 2026-10-26.

JBLControl doesn't collect, store or share any personal data. There's no account, no sign-in and no analytics or tracking of any kind, on Mac or Windows.

What the app does on its own device

JBLControl talks to your JBL speaker directly over Bluetooth LE — battery level, EQ presets and equalizer settings are read from and written to the speaker itself. This never leaves your computer and your speaker; it isn't sent to us or anyone else.

Your preferences (such as launch-at-login or your last-connected speaker) are stored only in your operating system's standard local settings (UserDefaults on Mac, the registry/local app data on Windows) and never transmitted anywhere.

What goes over the internet

JBLControl makes network requests to exactly two places, both read-only and neither requiring any personal information:

  • GitHub (api.github.com) — to check whether a newer version is available. This is a standard anonymous HTTPS request; it doesn't send any identifying information beyond what any web request includes (e.g. your IP address, inherent to the internet).
  • jbl.com — to download your speaker model's official product photo, so the app can show it. Only the model and color code are used to pick the right image.

Neither of these services is used for tracking or analytics, and JBLControl doesn't integrate any third-party analytics, advertising or tracking SDKs.

Diagnostics reports

If you choose Copy Diagnostics from the app's menu, it builds a text report (app version, speaker model, recent protocol traffic) and copies it to your clipboard — nothing is sent automatically. It's meant to be pasted into a GitHub issue you submit yourself, and the report already masks your speaker's MAC address and serial number before it's copied.

Children's privacy

JBLControl does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children, because it does not collect information from anyone at all.

Changes to this policy

If this policy ever changes, the update will be posted on this page with a new "last updated" date.
