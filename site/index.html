Features

Everything the JBL app does on your phone, on your computer.

Presets and a real equalizer

Switch between JBL's own Signature, Chill, Energetic and Vocal tunings, or shape your own sound with Custom and watch the response curve follow your sliders.

  • 7 bands125 Hz to 8 kHz on Flip 7 and Charge 6
  • ±6 dBper band, with the bass going down to −9 dB
  • 3 bandsbass, mid and treble on Charge 5 and similar

Battery at a glance

Percentage right in the menu bar or tray, green while charging, plus a heads-up when it drops to 20%.

Keep Awake

JBL speakers switch off after 10–20 silent minutes. Keep Awake stops that while your computer is playing to it.

Several speakers? One click.

Every JBL nearby shows up as a chip; pick one to switch. JBLControl remembers yours and reconnects by itself, even though JBL speakers change their Bluetooth address every few minutes.

No account. No tracking. Open source.

It talks to your speaker over Bluetooth and only goes online to check for updates and fetch speaker photos. Every line is on GitHub under the MIT license.

Install

Up and running in a minute.

  1. Download

    The DMG for Mac, or the installer for Windows (x64, or Arm64 for Snapdragon PCs).

  2. Install

    Mac: drag JBLControl onto Applications. Windows: run the installer; it doesn't need administrator rights.

  3. Allow it the first time

    JBLControl is free and not yet notarized by Apple or signed for Microsoft, so your system warns once. On Mac: System Settings → Privacy & Security → Open Anyway. On Windows: More info → Run anyway. Why this happens and why it's safe: on Mac, on Windows.

  4. Turn your speaker on

    JBLControl finds it by itself. If JBL Portable is open on your phone, close it; the speaker takes one control connection at a time.

FAQ

Good questions.

Is this an official JBL app?

No. JBLControl is an independent, open-source project, not affiliated with or endorsed by JBL or Harman. It speaks the same Bluetooth protocol as JBL's own app, building on community research by openjbl.

Why does my Mac or PC warn me about it?

Apple and Microsoft only let apps open without a warning if the developer pays for notarization or code signing. JBLControl is a free hobby project, so it isn't (yet). The warning means the system hasn't checked this build, not that it found anything wrong. All the code is public, and each release lists SHA-256 checksums so you can confirm your download.

Do I still need the JBL Portable app?

Only for firmware updates, PartyBoost and Auracast, which JBLControl doesn't do. Your EQ settings are stored on the speaker, so they apply wherever you play from.

Does Keep Awake drain the battery?

Yes, a little: the speaker stays on instead of switching itself off. It only works while your computer is the speaker's sound output, and the icon turns orange while it's on so you don't forget it.

My speaker isn't listed as verified. Will it work?

Probably for battery and info. For EQ on beta and experimental models, turn on "Allow experimental EQ" in Settings, and please send a device report so it can be verified for everyone.
